Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to copy the owner's own files to and from the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

  • No data is collected, stored or processed by this website or by any server operated for this client.
  • Google user data accessed through the Google Drive API is transferred only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. It is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone else.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
